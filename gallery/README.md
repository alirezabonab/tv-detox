The online slideshow reads `photos.txt` in order. To add a photo, place a 1920 × 1080 JPEG under 2 MiB in this directory, list its filename in `photos.txt`, and add its credit below. The TV refreshes the list when Astro Home starts and about every six hours while the slideshow runs.

These photos are available under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Each image is cropped and resized for the TV.
